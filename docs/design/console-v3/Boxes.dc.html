KWAZO console · the third look

Where a box opens

Four ways, chosen by what the box asks. The same on every page, so staff always know where to look. On a phone each takes the phone's own shape.
1 · Under its buttonOne to three questions, or a short answer to a press.
2 · From the rightA form of four fields or more. It scrolls as one page; Save stays at its foot.
3 · In the middleA question before a change to people or money. It names who changes and who doesn't.
4 · A page of its ownA big setup, in numbered parts with a summary that fills in.
1

Under its button

The box opens right under what was pressed, pushing the page down. What happened is said in that same place, in one sentence, when it is done. Examples: Add a tax rate, Refund sale, Set up, Remove a tax rate, Archive a discount.
Rules · Opens under the row or button pressed, never somewhere else on the page. · One main button and Cancel at its foot. · A question about one thing (Remove, Archive) sits here as a sentence with its button. · When it is done, the box closes and its sentence stays: "Sale voided: …", "Saved."

2

From the right

A panel slides in from the right and the page stays behind it. Examples: Add a product, Add a discount, a lead, Invite staff. On a phone it is a full page, with Save at its foot.
Rules · Title and a close X at the top; Save and Cancel always at the foot. · The panel scrolls as one page. · While something is changed: "You have changes that aren't saved." beside Save. · A refusal is said beside Save, with what was typed kept.

3

In the middle

The page darkens and the question sits in the middle. It names who will change and who won't, with the reason in one line each: a few names, then "and N more", See all. Examples: Void sale, Give a membership to the people on your list, Remove members, Send a message. On a phone it rises from the bottom.
Rules · The title is the question, ending in "?". · Who changes, ticked; who doesn't, with why. Nobody who was not ticked changes. · The main button says what it does and to how many: "Give Gold Weekly to 5 people". A destructive one is red and outlined. · Cancel or the X closes it with nothing changed.

4

A page of its own

A big setup leaves the list for a page with a back link, numbered parts, and a summary on the right that fills in as it is typed. Examples: Add a membership type, a challenge, a new business. On a phone the summary comes last and the main button stays at the foot.
Rules · "← Memberships" at the top goes back with nothing saved. · Parts are numbered 1, 2, 3, each a box with its title. · The summary says how it will read to a member: "You're adding". · A refusal: "Not saved yet. Fix what is marked in red above.", beside the button.
