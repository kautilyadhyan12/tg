Notes for the builder

Staff without check-in rights see “Who came in, by day.” under the title, no Check in box and no Remove. Staff who can check in but can't see the member list search by “Name or member number”. "Check someone in" reads Check in and "Checked in today" reads Today's check-ins, as the word list says; "Who came" reads Check-in history. The two arrows beside the day are named Previous day and Next day for screen readers; the whole day box opens the calendar. A name in Check-in history opens their visits under it, and pressing it again closes them. The time tags are the gym's own clock. Remove opens under its row (the four ways a box opens, Boxes, way 1).
