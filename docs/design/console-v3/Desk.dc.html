Notes for the builder

The desk is a page of its own, with no menu: it can only check people in. Its colours follow the light or dark look the device is set to. Green for checked in, gold for “see staff”, red for refused, grey for “try again”; the words always say which, never the colour alone. The words of a refusal (“This link has already been used…”) are the server's own. It names Settings in words because this device is signed in to nobody and cannot open the console. On a phone or a narrow desk the two buttons keep their names as labels for screen readers and show as icons.
