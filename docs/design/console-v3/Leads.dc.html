Notes for the builder

The list, its tick boxes and the bar of what is selected follow Members (V3a): tick the box at the top for the page shown, then Select all for every lead that matches. Email due and Email problems show only when there are some, or while picked. A row opens the lead from the right (LeadPanel). Your gym page opens its panel from the right (GymPage); Import leads opens in the middle (LeadImport). When the gym's account is read-only, Import leads and Add lead are switched off and the page says why, in the account's own sentence. On a phone, the selected bar sits at the foot of the screen with its tick box (PhoneLeads).
