Rules for every phone screen

The console’s twelve rules, said for a member holding a phone.

1

One screen, one job

The title, top left, says where you are. A screen has one main button.

2

Five tabs, always there

Today · Workout · Food · Gym · Me. Everything in the app is under one of them. A screen opened from a tab has a back arrow, top left.

3

The key number is the biggest thing

What you check most is big and bold. Its label is small and calm.

4

Orange means “press here”

One solid orange button in a box at most. A second action is pale orange; the rest are outlined.

5

Colour always has a meaning

Green is good, amber is “look at this”, red is a problem. Always with a word, never colour alone.

6

One font, a fixed set of sizes

Archivo everywhere, in seven sizes. Text grows when the phone’s own text size is set bigger, and nothing is cut off.

7

The same gaps everywhere

16 at the sides of the screen, 20 between boxes, 16 inside a box.

8

Big enough for a thumb

A main button is 48 tall and a row 56. Nothing you press is under 44. Pick from a list or a calendar instead of typing.

9

One pattern for pop-ups

A question or a choice opens from the bottom, under your thumb. A person or a thing opens as a full screen with a back arrow.

10

Nothing is lost without internet

A workout, a meal, a weigh-in or a run is kept on the phone and sent when the internet is back. A line at the top says so, and nobody is signed out.

11

Every screen in both looks

Dark and light, switched on Me, with Auto following the phone.

12

A member’s words

Say it the way a gym’s front desk would. An empty screen says what to do next, with its button.
