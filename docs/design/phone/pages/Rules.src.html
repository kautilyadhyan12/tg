The phone app’s look

The member web’s design, fitted to a phone

Same colours, same font, same pictures, same boxes in the same order.

1

The design is the member web’s

Near-black page, dark boxes with a thin line, orange for what you press, Inter. Dark only, as the web is.

2

The pictures stay

Every photo and drawing a web page has is on its phone screen, made small enough to open fast.

3

Same boxes, same order

A row of four on the web is two and two on the phone. A row of picture cards swipes sideways. Nothing is turned into one long list.

4

Five tabs at the bottom

The side menu’s pages sit under Today · Workout · Food · Gym · Me. The AI Coach is not in the phone app.

5

Big enough for a thumb

A main button is 48 tall. Nothing you press is under 44. No words smaller than 11.

6

A choice opens from the bottom

Adding a food, booking a class or a question opens as a box from the bottom of the screen.

7

Nothing is lost without internet

A workout, a meal or a run is kept on the phone and sent when the internet is back. A line at the top says so.
