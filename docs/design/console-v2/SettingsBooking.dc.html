Booking rules

Classes: opens 7 days before · free to cancel until 2 hours before · waitlist of 20. Personal training: opens 7 days before · free to cancel until 2 hours before.

A one-time charge, never monthly: added once to the member's bill each time they cancel after free cancelling ends. They see the fee before they confirm. 0 means no fee. Members on a class pack never pay it: the class stays used on their pack.
No-show fee
₹0
once, for each no-show
A one-time charge, never monthly: added once to the member's bill each time they are marked No-show, by staff, or by itself 15 minutes after the class ends when you check people in and they never checked in. Marking them Came takes an unpaid fee off. 0 means no fee. Class pack members never pay it.

Personal training

These apply to every personal training session from now on, the ones already booked too. Nobody's session is cancelled.
When booking opens
7
days before the session
Members can book a session from then until it starts. Your staff can book one for a member further ahead.
Free cancelling
Until
2
hours
before the session
After that it is a late cancel: a pack keeps the session as used, and it still counts where a membership has a limit on sessions. Your staff can give the session back.
Late cancel fee
₹0
once, for each late cancel
A one-time charge, never monthly: added once to the member's bill each time a session is cancelled after free cancelling ends and stays used. They see the fee before they confirm. 0 means no fee. A session your staff give back costs nothing. Members on a personal training pack never pay it: the session stays used on their pack.
No-show fee
₹0
once, for each no-show
A one-time charge, never monthly: added once to the member's bill each time staff mark them No-show. Marking them Came takes an unpaid fee off. 0 means no fee. Members on a personal training pack never pay it.
