- **Worst thing:** a photo sent turned the wrong way, so the food is read wrong. First test: "the worst thing: the meal camera never turns a photo by the phone's tilt, for a meal, a label or a barcode" (`apps/mobile/test/screens/foodPhoto.test.tsx`).
- **Cause, read in expo-camera 57.0.6's Android code:** `ExpoCameraView`'s `OrientationEventListener` sets the photo's turn from the tilt sensor on every reading and keeps the last one when the phone is flat (`ORIENTATION_UNKNOWN`); with `exif: false` the camera turns the pixels itself and says nothing of it, so the app cannot undo it. The iPhone keeps the screen's picture unless `responsiveOrientationWhenOrientationLocked` is true; Android had no such setting.
- **Built:** `patches/expo-camera@57.0.6.patch` gives Android that setting (off: the listener changes nothing and the turn is the screen's); `apps/mobile/package.json` builds expo-camera from source (`expo.autolinking.android.buildFromSource`), since Expo ships it ready-built (`local-maven-repo`) and a patch to its Kotlin would never reach the app; `patches/*.patch -text` keeps the patch's LF ends on a Windows checkout. The meal camera passes `false`; the gym post camera passes `true` (unchanged on Android). `PlateCorners` removed with its tests; the barcode box stays. `pnpm patch-commit` failed on Windows' path length in the scratchpad, so the patch was written with `git diff --no-index` and declared by hand; the lock file changed by 7 lines.
