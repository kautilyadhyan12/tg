- **Worst thing:** a lost or lent phone stays signed in after its owner signs in on a new one. The first test in `apps/api/test/auth.phone.test.ts`.
- **Built** (spec Part 3 §10.5, with a "Built in 12a" paragraph): migration `0105_phone_sessions` (`refresh_tokens.client` `web`·`phone`, `revoked_reason`, both CHECKed). `POST /v1/auth/code/verify` with `client: "phone"` answers `{ user, isNewAccount, tokens }` and sets no cookie. `POST /v1/auth/refresh` and `/logout` with `{ refreshToken }` in the body are the phone's. `repo.startPhoneSession`: under the user's row lock, one transaction ends that person's other live phone sign-ins with `signed_in_elsewhere` and writes the new one. The old phone's refresh is a 401 `signed_in_elsewhere` with its sentence (`SIGNED_IN_ELSEWHERE`, `@app/shared`), and ends nothing more. A phone's token as a cookie, or a browser's in a body, is a plain 401 and ends nothing.
