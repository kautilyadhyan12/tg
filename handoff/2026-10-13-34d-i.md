- **Why this job:** items 31, 32b, 33 and 34b are phone screens and wait for feature folder 1's item 12. 34d comes before item 36 and its first part needs no phone; it was split in three (ROADMAP). **Ordinary.** Kd: *"go"*. **Built at Opus 5.5 medium** (his `/model` line before "go"; xhigh was recommended and said).
- **Built, no screen:** `GET /v1/nutrition/suggestions` (`modules/nutrition/suggest.ts`, pure; `service.getMealIdeas`). For each meal still to come today it answers three ideas: a plate (a protein food, a grain or potato, a vegetable), a dish with what is eaten beside it (bread beside a soup or a salad, rice or roti beside a curry, a grain or potato beside a stew, fruit after a dish that is a meal by itself), and a bowl or toast with fruit. Each food is on the app's own list, at an amount of its own serving, with the list's numbers, so an idea logged by canonical and grams is saved at the calories it showed (tested through the route). The reply's shape is `mealIdeasResponseSchema` in `@app/shared`.
- **The rules:** the calories are the rings' own (so a plan that may not be cut is not cut here), less what is logged today on the person's own day; shared across the meals a day not yet logged. No idea under 150 kcal or over 700, none past one and a half usual meals, six meals at most; under 300 kcal a meal is a bowl with fruit, fruit with nuts, or toast with a spread. A row logged as a snack, or under 150 kcal, is eaten and is not a meal. An idea is within a fifth of its meal's calories, holds 750 g of food at most and is in the reply once, or is left out (none was, in the 2,688 days the test tries for each diet); where no dish or bowl of the diet can be sized to a meal, a plate takes its place. The same person gets the same ideas all day and other ones the next day (drawn from the person's id and the day, no randomness and no clock in the rule).
- **Only these foods are suggested:** 109 named in the pools at the top of `suggest.ts`: plain meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, grains, vegetables, fruit, nuts and 27 home-style dishes. Fried food, sweets, fast food, alcohol and sugary drinks stay in the search and are never offered (RULINGS 2026-09-12). The terminal's own choice of foods, Kd's to change.
- **Worst thing, first test** (`nutrition.suggest.unit.test.ts`): a vegan or vegetarian offered meat, fish, egg or milk. Every food that may be suggested is held to its own table's words (the three fixtures of 34c), by a word list written in the test that includes words no suggested food has; then every idea of 2,688 days for each diet. One break by hand, put back: the diet no longer filtering the foods turned the three diet tests RED ("Trout (cooked)", "Egg (hard-boiled)" offered to a vegan).
