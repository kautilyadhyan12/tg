- **Built, no screen:** `GET /v1/nutrition/suggestions` (`modules/nutrition/suggest.ts`, pure; `service.getMealIdeas`). For each meal still to come today it answers three ideas: a plate (a protein food, a starch, a vegetable), a dish with something beside it, and a bowl or toast with fruit. Each food is on the app's own list, at an amount of its own serving, with the list's numbers, so an idea logged by canonical and grams is saved at the calories it showed (tested through the route). The reply's shape is `mealIdeasResponseSchema` in `@app/shared`.
- **The rules:** the calories are the rings' own (so a plan that may not be cut is not cut here), less what is logged today on the person's own day; shared across the meals a day not yet logged. No idea under 150 kcal or over 800, none past one and a half usual meals, six meals at most; under 300 kcal a meal is fruit with yogurt or nuts. An idea is within a fifth of its meal's calories or is left out (none was, in the 2,688 days the test tries for each diet). The same person gets the same ideas all day and other ones the next day (drawn from the person's id and the day, no randomness and no clock in the rule).
- **Only these foods are suggested:** 106 named in the pools at the top of `suggest.ts`: plain meat, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, grains, vegetables, fruit, nuts and 28 home-style dishes. Fried food, sweets, fast food, alcohol and sugary drinks stay in the search and are never offered (RULINGS 2026-09-12). The terminal's own choice of foods, Kd's to change.
- **Worst thing, first test** (`nutrition.suggest.unit.test.ts`): a vegan or vegetarian offered meat, fish, egg or milk.
