# 2026-11-06 · Stage 5 item 37b-i (feature folder 2): The map under the run (branch `run-map`)

Kd's decisions and the terminal's own: `rulings/2026-11-06-37b-i.md` (OpenFreeMap through MapLibre, the switch, no Google and no satellite, the map first in a run, light by day and dark at night, exact stones).

- **Why this job:** the next of item 37 (Kd, 2026-11-06: running before anything else). 37b split in two: the map, then hills climbed (37b-ii). **Risky** (it sends where the person is to a map company); Opus xhigh recommended, built at Opus 5.5 xhigh and max, as set.
- **Worst thing:** a map company learning who runs where. First tests: `apps/mobile/test/runMap.test.ts` (each map look asks only the map's own addresses; the run's trail, stones and ends are shapes, never an address) and the map group of `test/screens/running.test.tsx` (the live, summary and full maps given nothing but what they draw; GET /v1/geo/map carries no body or query; no request but Save carries the run's points).
- **Built, server:** `GET /v1/geo/map` (signed in; the same answer for everyone, worked out at start, no database) says which map to draw: OpenFreeMap's, or with `MAP_SOURCE=protomaps`, `MAP_PROTOMAPS_FILE` and `MAP_PROTOMAPS_GLYPHS` our own copy of Protomaps' world file (refused at start with one missing, or http in production). `RUNBOOK/switch-the-map.md`.
- **Built, phone:** @maplibre/maplibre-react-native 11.5.0 (MIT, published 2026-10-04; its twelve new packages MIT, ISC or BSD, none runs a script at install; @types/geojson for its types). The run opens on its map (Kd), following its front with the 37a beam until the person moves the map (Follow me); Numbers beside Pause gives the big numbers, Map takes them back. After Finish the Route box is the run's map (still, tap to open it full size, pinch and move, the cross back); "The map shows when you're online." when the server could not be reached. The map's look is the app's own (`look/colours.ts` `mapColours`, `trailColours`; `mapStyle.ts`): a light full map with landmarks from sunrise to sunset where the run is (NOAA's sun, `daylight.ts`), the mid grey after; Map colours on the Running page (Automatic, Light, Dark). The kept map source is `run-map.json`. Stones stand at the exact metre (`unitMarkPoints` in `runMaths.ts`), as the splits count.
- **Not on an iPhone yet:** item 21 adds MapLibre's iPhone setup with the iPhone build.
