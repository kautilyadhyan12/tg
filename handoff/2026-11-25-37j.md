- **Built:** `autoPause.ts`. A point's feel comes from `run.motion` (`feelAt`: the newest second at or before it, at most 3 s old; under 0.71 m/s² still, over 2.27 moving). **Running:** 4 still points over 3 s pause, dated to the first; a run of them ends at seconds the phone cannot read for more than 3 s, or 8 s with no point. **Paused, still:** on wheels only after a steady rise of `max(40, 4 × accuracy)` from the stop, judged every 5 s (back at most half the accuracy, on at most 5 m/s plus that), dated to the last point within `max(3, accuracy / 4)` of the stop; the stop's place is averaged over still points within half the accuracy. **Paused, moving:** 3 moving points, 10 s after the pause began, 8 m (or half the accuracy) farther from the stop than 10 s before, dated to the first but never more than 10 s back; or 40 m (4 × accuracy) from the stop for a slow walker under a weak signal; 8 in a row with fewer than two placed points. The place rules are as before. With every feel unknown, the answers are the old rule's over the three real runs, the hike and the indoor walk (literals taken before the change). `recorder.ts`: feel on each point. A resume at or before its pause's start removes the pause. `pausedAt` is known after a reopen and after the person's own Pause. On wheels is kept through Pause and Resume (taken at Pause for the person's own pause, the rule's own for an Auto-pause, never from another run). `PAUSED_TAIL` is 300, and heart readings in a pause that is undone are kept (`pausedHeart`). "Auto-paused." and "Run resumed." are not said for a stop beginning in the first 15 s (`START_HUSH_MS`).
