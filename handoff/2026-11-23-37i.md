Kd's decisions and the terminal's own: `rulings/2026-11-23-37i.md`.

- **Why:** brief 2 of the four from the running bug hunt (feature folder 2's `reviews/run-still-2-motion-sensor-brief.md`): position alone cannot tell standing from a slow walk once the GPS is unsure; a phone on somebody walking is shaken at every step. Strava's help page says its run Auto-pause is *"motion-based (accelerometer)"*, its rides' GPS-based. **Ordinary**; nothing shown, nothing sent.
- **Built:** `apps/mobile/modules/run-motion` (Kotlin, no permission): the wake-up accelerometer at 25 a second on its own thread, each second's standard deviation of the acceleration's size handed over as `{ at, sd, n }` (under 10 readings not given; 900 kept at most); `runMotionNative.ts`; `recorder.ts` keeps `LiveRun.motion` (`[ms into the run, sd × 100]`) from Go, paused too, drained before each batch of points, in `run-now-motion-N.json`, read back after the app closed, removed at Save and Discard. `phoneMotionSchema`, `keptRunMotionSchema` and `motionFiles` in `packages/shared/src/geo.ts`. The tool: `apps/mobile/tools/run-motion-measure.mjs <data folder>`.
- **Measured on 75 people** (MotionSense 24, iPhone in a front pocket, read at 25 a second; WISDM 2019 51, Android in a pocket, 20 a second; HAPT left out, see rulings). Percentiles 1/5/50/95/99 in m/s²: standing 0.01/0.02/0.04/0.31/0.84; sitting 0.01/0.01/0.03/0.18/0.59; walking 1.76/2.33/4.44/7.18/8.89; stairs 0.90/1.58/3.52/6.43/8.44; jogging 3.68/4.89/7.06/13.98/16.90. Each person's lowest/middle/highest: run the tool.
- **The limits, set from all people but one, each left out in turn (the rule is written in the tool before any is left out):** `FEEL_STILL_BELOW` **0.71**, `FEEL_MOVING_ABOVE` **2.27** (the planner had guessed 0.35 and 0.9). (a) 4 seconds on foot in a row all still: failed for 3 of 75 (WISDM 1620 and 1650 on stairs, 1627 the last 8 s of a jog), all read as stops; Kd kept the limits. (b) standing read still 9 seconds in 10: under it for WISDM 1644 (85.8%) and 1648 (89.2%), single restless seconds. Standing read moving 3 seconds in a row: never for 72 people, 1 to 3 times for 3.
