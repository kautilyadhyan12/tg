# 2026-11-24 · Stage 5 item 37l (feature folder 2): A run keeps recording with the screen off (branch `run-screen-off`)

Kd's decisions and the terminal's own: `rulings/2026-11-24-37l.md`.

- **Why:** 37i measured that with the screen off a run lost its location (0 fixes in 6 minutes on the store KWAZO) and thought the app's own service stopped itself. **Ordinary**; nothing sent.
- **The cause, measured on Kd's moto g35 (Android 15), 2026-11-24:** not the app's code. The phone sets this app (and 25 of the 40 downloaded apps checked) to **Restricted**; Android then takes the run's service off foreground at once (`am_foreground_service_stop` STOP_FOREGROUND 86 ms to 3.3 s after Start, `isForeground=false`, `get-bg-restriction-level` = `background_restricted`). The same store build with the limit lifted (`cmd appops set com.kwazo.app RUN_ANY_IN_BACKGROUND allow` and `cmd deviceidle whitelist +`): service foreground for the whole run, **467 location batches in 467 seconds with the screen off**, 60 a minute for 8 minutes. 37i's "65 ms" run had its lift silently undone: **this phone resets the app to Restricted each time the app starts afresh** (the log shows "toggled into fg service restriction" at that start; a force-stop does it too).
- **Built:** `apps/mobile/modules/run-battery` (Kotlin, no permission): `restricted()` = `ActivityManager.isBackgroundRestricted`, `open()` = `android.settings.VIEW_ADVANCED_POWER_USAGE_DETAIL` for the app, App info as the fallback; `runBatteryNative.ts`; `BatteryLimit.tsx` (`useBatteryLimited`, checked while the Running page shows and again when the app comes back to the front; `BatteryLimitRow`), first row of the Running page's settings box while limited. Verified on the phone: Android answers `background_restricted` and the row shows; the button opens "App battery usage" for Feature 2; the row reads whole at the largest text (font scale 1.5), a small phone (720x1520 at 320) and an opened folding phone (1812x2176 at 420).
- **Verified (commands):** mobile `tsc` clean; `eslint` on the changed files clean; `test/screens/runBattery.test.tsx` 9 passed; `running.test.tsx` 64 passed; `test/copy.test.ts` 30 passed (vitest). The worst-thing test first: a limited phone is told on the page with START; tables for limited, not limited, no such code, a failing answer (false warnings are worse than none: only "limited" shows one); the button falls back to the app's settings; the line goes when the person comes back and returns if the phone puts the limit back.
- **What the phone does that the app cannot fix (told to Kd):** (1) the limit is put back whenever the app starts afresh, and a restricted app is closed quickly once the person leaves it (leaving for Settings closed the development copy; the new process started Restricted again); the row says so again on the next look. (2) Even with the limit lifted, the phone's memory manager (`lowmemorykiller … thrashing`) closed the 260 MB development copy 20 s after the screen went off, then closed it again at each restart: 13 points and 15 motion seconds in 570 dark seconds, against 467 of 467 for the lighter store build. A screen-off walk of a development copy on this phone measures the phone.
- **Open:** (a) the wake-up accelerometer question from 37i (needed with the screen off?) is not answered: the store app has no motion code yet, and the development copy is closed by the phone.
