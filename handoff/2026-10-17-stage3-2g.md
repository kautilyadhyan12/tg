# Handoff · 2026-10-17 · Stage 3 item 2g, a limit on creating gyms (web folder 2)

- **Why:** Kd asked what stops a hacker creating 100 or 200 gyms in a minute. Free trials were held by 2b and 2c, but one account could create gyms without end and as fast as it liked.
- **Built:** `POST /v1/orgs` takes five gyms an hour per account and twenty per address (`orgs_create`), and gives a press back when the form is refused (`giveBackRateLimit`), so only gyms created count. `createOrgAttempt` counts the owner's gyms with no `paid_at` plan under an advisory lock on the owner and refuses the eleventh (`unpaid_gym_limit`, 403): "You already have 10 gyms or studios with no paid plan. Choose a plan for one of them to create another." with the `SUPPORT_EMAIL` line when one is set. New gym shows **Your organisations** under it. `OrgRouteOverrides.gymLimits` lets eight older test files that make many gyms for one owner raise the limits.
- **Verified:** `tsc` and `eslint` for `api`, eslint for the two web files, all 0; `orgs.gymLimits.test.ts` 7 of 7 and `orgs.routes.test.ts` 156 of 156 on `aihg_web2`; the whole api suite: 254 files passed in one run, the 37 others (eight hit the new limits and were given `gymLimits`, the rest ran out of the local Postgres's 100 connections) passed three at a time; web `planPrompt` 39 of 39 and `console` render tests. Walked in headless Edge (`apps/api/.cost/look-2g.mjs`): the eleventh unpaid gym is refused with the sentence and Your organisations, which opens the list; Cancel lands there too; a sixth gym in an hour gets the hour's sentence; both fit at 390 px. Cost: the count took 5.8 ms with 20,082 gyms (EXPLAIN ANALYZE, in a transaction rolled back), once per Create.
