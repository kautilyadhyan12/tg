# 2026-11-25 · Stage 5 item 37k (feature folder 2): the line holds still with Auto-pause off (branch `run-still-auto-pause-off`, PR #649)

Kd's decisions and the terminal's own: `rulings/2026-11-25-37k.md`.

- **Why:** brief 4 of the running bug hunt (feature folder 2's `reviews/run-still-4-auto-pause-off-brief.md`; hunt A1): with Auto-pause off, the line and the distance grew while the person stood, unless the phone was sure to 10 m, and then only after 20 s. Kd added the clock that stepped back at an Auto-pause (seen 0:19, then 0:16). **Risky** (the saved run sends something new). **Worst thing:** with Auto-pause off, real metres left out because the phone felt still. First test: the pram, Auto-pause off; the phone and the server count the same, never less than the same stops with Auto-pause on.
- **Built (phone):** with Auto-pause off, 37j's rule opens and closes `LiveRun.still` (`openStill`, `closeStill`; the person's Pause, Auto-pause turned on and Finish close an open stretch; a reopened app judges an open one as a stop). With Auto-pause on, the clock stops where it stands (`pauseAt`, even for a late batch, whose points from before the pause stay the run's), and the seconds back to the stop are `LiveRun.datedBack`. `sumsAt` gives the screen, the voice and the guide one sum. The guide reads the screen's clock (`clockAt` for "begun after the stop"; `guideBack` is gone). The summary sends `still` only when there is one.
- **Built (maths and server):** `runSums(…, felt)`, `RUN_CALC_VERSION` 4. `stillHeld` honours a stretch from its first point to its last: two points at least, its end point left out where the person moved off, every point within `travelMetresAt` (now in the shared maths) of the first, going along under 1 m/s (`trendSpeed`). `heldApart` cuts the piece there as a pause does, and its time goes into `stillMs`. Standing spans are read forward once (`Spans`). `saveRunRequestSchema` and the climb request take optional `still` (at most `RUN_MAX_PAUSES`, in order, none overlapping, none empty, none past the end); the list count leaves room; nothing is kept. Policy 3.10: one line on the times a run stood still; `LEGAL_UPDATED_ON` 2026-11-25.
