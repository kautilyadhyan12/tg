- **Verified:** web `frontPage.test.js` 16, `clipRules.test.js` 24; lint clean; the build; 29 shapes through the server's own settings in Docker, nothing cut (short laptop screens keep two or three stickers under the bar while it shows, as before); WebKit at iPhone 15, iPhone SE, iPhone sideways, iPad both ways and a Mac: no error, nothing blocked, no sideways scroll, both fonts, every feature supported, Lexend's weights 946 / 962 / 977 / 991 as in Edge.
