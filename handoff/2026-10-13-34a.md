- **Where each food's figures come from:** a USDA food, its own row · one of our list, the USDA entry it cites (its fibre the list's own) · a CoFID food, its fibre alone · a packaged product, its label (`labelMore`, `openfoodfacts.adapter.ts`) · a scan's estimate and a food priced by the person's own numbers, none. A meal saved before this job has none and still reads.
- **The rule:** a figure nobody measured is "not known" (`null`), never 0, and a sum says how many foods gave none (`notKnown`). Open Food Facts keeps every figure in grams, so sodium and the minerals are turned to milligrams once, in `labelMore`.
