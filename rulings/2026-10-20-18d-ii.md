## Pricing, trials and money

- 2026-10-20 · **Standard practice only, nothing invented** (Kd: *"ALL I NEED IS STANDARD PRACTISE NO NEW INVENTED THIGS FROM OUR END"*; earlier *"please take responsibility do not depend on me"*). Kd's target is **small independent owner-run businesses** (*"MY TARGET IS NOT BIG FITNESS BUSINESS BUT SMALL INDIPENDENT OWNER BUSINESSES"*), in the US, Canada and India first. For them a saved card charged monthly is the usual way in the US: at PushPress's US gyms, bank (ACH) payments are 9.4 % of member payment volume.
- 2026-10-20 · **18d-ii's plan, agreed** (Kd: *"WILL BUILD IN A MEW TERMIAL , I WILL SAY BUI;D THE NEXT THING"*, after the plan below was final):
  - **At the desk:** staff holding the payments tick (`billing.members`) press **Add card** on a person's page; a box under it shows **Square's own card boxes** (Square's window inside our page; the number goes from the browser to Square, never to us). Staff or the member type the card; **the member ticks the agreement themselves**, which takes the place of Square's signed form: "I agree that {gym} may save this card and charge it for my membership and any fees when they are due. I can remove it at the front desk at any time." Words, time and whose screen are kept. Square checks the card with the bank where the country requires it; then "Card saved: Visa ending 1111." in the same place. Kd: *"THAT IT THEY WRITE THESES THING IN SQUARE NOT IN OURS THAT IS GOOD"*.
  - **On the phone:** the member adds or updates their own card on Square's form, with the same tick. This job builds the server part; the phone draws it (Stage 5 item 20).
  - **What staff see:** only staff with the payments tick see "Card on file: Visa ending 1111 · expires 12/2028", with Replace card and Remove card, or "No card on file" with Add card and "Tara can add a card in the KWAZO app." Other staff see nothing of the card (Kd asked *"should staff see these sensetive things"*; the card rules allow the last four digits, as Mindbody and PushPress show).
  - **One card a person;** a new one replaces the old. **Only staff remove a card** (PushPress and Mindbody: only the business removes it); the member adds or updates.
  - **No "your card was saved" message:** only Square's own till sends one; no gym app found doing it, and Square's developer rules do not ask for it.
  - **Not this job:** charging the card (18d-iii), refunds (18d-iv), saving a card on a Square card machine (Square's Terminal saves cards only in the US, Canada and Australia; not planned), a US bank account through Square (Square switches recurring bank charges on per app when asked, US only; not planned: under a tenth of payments at small US gyms).
- 2026-10-20 · **India is Razorpay with UPI, never Square** (Kd: *"for india it is razor pay through upi not square"*; *"if memebrship payment is one time then scaning is ok"*): UPI AutoPay for monthly fees (approved once with the UPI PIN; the member is told 24 hours before each debit; above ₹15,000 the PIN each time), Razorpay's page for paying once, a UPI QR code or cash at the desk recorded at Sell. Razorpay (18f) waits on Razorpay accepting Kd's partner sign-up (`rulings/2026-10-19-18d-i.md`).
