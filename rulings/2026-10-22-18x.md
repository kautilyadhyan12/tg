## Pricing, trials and money

- 2026-10-22 · **Change membership, as Kd said go to it** (Kd: *"go B"*): staff change a membership in use for another on the price list, today or on its next payment date; today, the days already paid for and not yet used come off the new membership's first bill, before tax; no joining fee on a change.
- 2026-10-22 · **A cheaper move waits for the next payment date, so no money is ever left over** (Kd: *"go B"*, after asking for the industry's practice: Podplay schedules a downgrade for the next billing date with $0.00 due; MyTime changes on the next charge date; Glofox's own page says it "delays revenue reductions on downgrades"; none refunds what is left over, and account credit is after launch).
- 2026-10-22 · **The terminal's own, Kd's to overrule:** the credit is worked out from the money that stands on each paid period's bill (its tax added on top, joining fee and refunds left out), rounded down to the cent · a membership with a payment due, a frozen one, one not yet started, a class pack and a trial are not changed this way; the box says what to do instead, with its button · a same-price repeating move on the first day starts paid with no bill; a paid-once one at exactly the credit is refused · no discount or Refer a friend gift on a change · only staff who record payments can change one · classes and personal training booked on the old one move to the new one where it covers them; the rest end only after the box names them · the old one's tag reads "Changed".
