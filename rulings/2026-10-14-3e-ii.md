- 2026-10-14 · **Is signing on a screen standard and legal, and is it legal when the person has no app and staff open the screen?** Kd asked both before *"go"*. Told: a typed name and a tick is standard and legal in the US (ESIGN, UETA), Canada, the UK, the EU (eIDAS art. 25), Australia, New Zealand and India (IT Act s10A); what a waiver may exclude differs by country and is the gym's words and the lawyer's (Stage 4 item 3); at the desk the person signs themselves on staff's screen, as with paper, and staff typing for them would not count. Research, not advice. No change to the plan; the screen says "Hand this screen to {name}. They read the waiver and sign it themselves." and records which staff member opened it.
- 2026-10-14 · **A desk signature whose record is deleted for good is removed six years later**, as an app signature is six years after its account is deleted. Asked by round one (a terminal's choice had kept it with no end). Kd: *"yes"*. Built in 3e-iii.
