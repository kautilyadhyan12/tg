- 2026-10-13 · **"Biscuit", "flapjack", "scampi" and "kebab" alone name no food.** The terminal's own. Each is on the list under the longer name that says which ("American biscuit (baked)", "Oat flapjack (British oat bar)", "Breaded scampi (British)", "Shish kebab (lamb)"), as "jelly" and "chips" already were.
