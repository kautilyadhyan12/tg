## Privacy and legal

- 2026-10-23 · **No lawyer, now or later: our own documents are written by us before launch** (Kd: *"i do not hvae lawyer and wont be able to have one"*; *"why not we inlcude the things the others are doing"*). Our terms for businesses and the data agreement start from Common Paper's free agreements, written by *"a committee of expert attorneys"* and *"free to use and modify"* (CC BY 4.0); the terms of use and the privacy policy are our own words, holding the protections the big apps use (never copied: their text is copyrighted), each law's own list (India DPDP s5, GDPR art 13, PIPEDA, Washington RCW 19.373, CalOPPA), and what our code really does. **Amends RULINGS 2026-09-07 ("lawyer-reviewed terms") and every line there that waits for a lawyer; ROADMAP Stage 4 items 3 and 15.**
- 2026-10-23 · **No insurance before launch** (Kd: *"i ama indie dev men"*). No law read asks it of an app; Indian insurers' own pages call it not mandatory but for insurance sellers. Looked at again when the app earns money or a business's contract asks for it. **Amends RULINGS 2026-09-07 ("product liability insurance").**
- 2026-10-23 · **The app is open to individual users worldwide from launch, and Europe's representative (the EU and the UK) is added once Europe brings users or money** (Kd: *"i agree"*, after *"many indie devs are developing futness apps meal scan apps … i am sure they are not paying 652 a year bro"*). Read that day: Cal AI's privacy policy named none from its first saved copy (October 2024) until its 21 May 2026 version, which names DataRep; Bevel and SnapCalorie name none; MacroFactor names VeraSafe and a UK one. Said once: the law has no small-developer exception (GDPR art 27; the EDPB reads "occasional" as *"not carried out regularly"*); regulators mostly act on complaints, which a clear privacy policy, a delete button and an answer to every privacy email keep away. *The terminal's own:* the countries whose law needs an office or local storage (China, Russia) are checked with the documents before launch and put to Kd then.
- 2026-10-23 · **The app does keep health data** (Kd had said *"no health data is stored in our app just basic things"*): the health question (`user_health_screenings`, which our code calls *"Health data in the broad sense"*), weight and measurements, meals and run routes (Washington's law: *"Individual health conditions"*, *"measurements"*). The consent screens (Stage 4 item 3) cover it; nothing is removed. Groq is only the meal scanner's spare in the code and does nothing without its key (Kd: *"Groq is not used"*), so the privacy policy names only what is switched on.
- 2026-10-23 · **Correction to `rulings/2026-10-22-18r.md`:** Mindbody's *"You remain solely responsible for … compliance with all applicable consumer protection … laws"* is from its clause on its AI front-desk helper (2.16) only. Its general protections are its indemnity (10.1: the business's *"goods and services … including … personal injury, death"*) and its consumer agreement (the businesses, *"and not Mindbody, are responsible for … cancellation, return and refund policies"*); Glofox's terms carry the clearest one (*"IT SHALL BE YOUR SOLE AND EXCLUSIVE RESPONSIBILITY TO PREPARE THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS"*).
