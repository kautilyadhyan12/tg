## Workouts and the camera

- 2026-11-22 · **The terminal's own, his to overrule:** how deep a squat must go is set from the form standard, never from how shallow the people of the videos went: the knees past a quarter squat (110 degrees, a 2024 scoping review; his research file asks for about 90) plus the camera's own room on that reading, measured on the videos (2.78 degrees in 99 reps of 100): 113; the hips at least what a leg bent so allows (1 - sin 56.5 degrees = 0.166 of a leg): 0.16. Every proper squat in every video reads 107 degrees or deeper; the kettlebell push press (120 to 127) counts 0.
- 2026-11-22 · **The terminal's own, his to overrule:** the push press's dips that went most of the way are told "Go lower to count.", as a quarter squat is: the squat's movement does not read the arms, so to the camera they are one. It is the one other movement whose video carries a word; every other says nothing.
- 2026-11-22 · **The terminal's own, his to overrule:** the words a rep not counted carries: "Go lower to count." (a rep that tried and fell short), "Keep your feet in view." and "Keep your whole body in view." (what the camera could not see). Another movement, or a dip that never tried, says nothing, whatever the camera could not see.
- 2026-11-22 · **The terminal's own, his to overrule:** a rep is back up once the person stands tall again (both knees and the trunk read, each within 20 degrees, the hips still for 0.2 s), however low the hips read against the first standing; and a rep never begins while the person stands tall.
- 2026-11-22 · **The terminal's own, his to overrule:** a squat jack done slowly still counts as squats (2 at 1.5 times as slowly, 1 at twice): measured, the feet's stillness before a rep cannot tell it from a real squat (4 cm against 3.8), so it stays open for 52c, where jumps are counted as a kind of their own.
