# Rulings · 2026-10-20 · 1g, Keep my plan for a Paddle plan set to end (web folder 2)

## Money

- 2026-10-20 · **A Paddle plan set to end is kept from the Plan box**, as a Razorpay plan is: Keep my plan takes the cancel back at Paddle until three hours before the plan ends, and the reminder email then says "To keep it, press Keep my plan before {time}." A plan that renews shows and sends nothing new. Kd: *"go"*, to the plan, after asking why a plan that renews needs it (answered: only a plan set to end gets the button or the email).
- 2026-10-20 · **A plan handed over with its gym is never kept**, by the new owner or the old one; a hand-over that lands while Paddle is being asked sets the plan to end again. Kd asked whether the new owner pressing Keep charges the old owner (answered: no) and what happens when the old owner cancels on the 30th and hands the gym over on the 31st (answered: paid to its end, then it ends; the new owner chooses his own plan on his own card; nobody can keep it).
- 2026-10-20 · **The terminal's own, Kd's to overrule:** the button reads "Keep my plan", beside Manage payment, under the line "This plan is set to end. You can keep it until {time}." · after the press the box reads the plan's next payment, as a kept Razorpay plan does · Paddle down: "Paddle couldn't be reached to keep your plan. Please try again in a moment." · a plan with a payment owed is not kept here: "A payment didn't go through, so this plan can't be kept here. Press Update payment method to pay it."
