- 2026-11-06 · *The terminal's own, his to overrule:* the voice is this app's own Android code (`apps/mobile/modules/run-voice`), not expo-speech, which asks Android for no audio focus (music plays on at full volume over it) and shuts its voice down when the app's screen ends (a run swiped away goes quiet), read in its 57.0.3 source. The music is lowered while a line is said, a voice kept on the phone is taken over one that needs the internet, and the phone's own English is used (American English where the phone is in another language). The iPhone's voice comes with the iPhone (item 21).
- 2026-11-06 · *The terminal's own, his to overrule:* what is said, as Strava and Nike Run Club do: each whole kilometre or mile in the person's units, "2 kilometres. Time 11 minutes 20 seconds. Last kilometre 5 minutes 40 seconds. Average pace 5 minutes 40 seconds per kilometre." (the first mark without a last of its own; a pace slower than an hour a unit not said); "Auto-paused.", "Run paused.", "Run resumed."; nothing at Start or Finish, where the person is looking at the screen. Voice is on until turned off, on the Running page under Auto-pause.
- 2026-11-06 · *The terminal's own, from four real runs recorded on phones (three of them are the tests' fixture):* a mark is said 15 seconds after it is passed, with its numbers as they stood at the mark, because the route's line is smoothed with the points that follow; said at once, up to 9 marks in 12 were a second off the summary's, and 15 seconds after, none of 58. At a pause (Finish is pressed from one) it is said at once. Two passed at once (a gap in the signal) say only the newer; marks passed before the voice was turned on, before the app was reopened, or before the units were changed, are never said late.
