Common questions

What you need to know.

Does KWAZO record my workout video?

No. Exercise analysis takes place on your phone while you use the camera. KWAZO does not record, store or transmit your workout camera's video or images.

How do I invite my members?

Import your member list or add members individually, then select Invite. Each member receives an email invitation and can find your gym in the app.

Which devices support KWAZO?

The KWAZO app is coming to Android first, followed by iPhone. Gyms can access the console through a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

Is KWAZO a medical app?

No. KWAZO provides fitness, workout and nutrition guidance. It is not a medical app or a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Who can use KWAZO?

KWAZO is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can request account deletion at any time through the app's settings or our Delete your account page.
