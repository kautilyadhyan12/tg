## Full description (4000 at most)

KWAZO is your fitness and nutrition app. Log what you eat, record your runs, and keep up with your gym or studio, all in one place.

FOOD
• Log a meal in seconds: search for a food, scan its barcode, or read its label.
• Take a photo of your plate, and KWAZO finds the foods and their calories for you to check.
• See your calories, protein, carbs and fat for the day and the week.
• Meal ideas and meal plans that fit your goal.

RUNNING
• Record your runs with GPS, even with the screen locked, and see your route on a map.
• Pace, distance and elevation for every run.
• Connect a heart rate monitor for your live heart rate and five heart rate zones.
• A voice that tells you how you are doing after each kilometre or mile.
• A run plan built around your days and your goal.

YOUR GYM OR STUDIO
• Your membership pass on your phone, even with no signal.
• Book classes and personal training, and join the waitlist when a class is full.
• Your membership, payments and receipts. Add a card or pay what you owe.
• Updates, events, challenges and the leaderboard from your gym, studio or box.
• Messages from your gym, and a notification when a place opens up.

YOUR DAY
• A Dashboard with your goals, streaks and badges.
• Workout and meal reminders at the times you choose.

PRIVACY
• Meal photos are used to find the foods and are then discarded. KWAZO never keeps them.
• Your runs are yours. Your gym or studio never sees your routes or your heart rate.
• No ads, and your data is never sold.
• Delete your account at any time in the app.

KWAZO is a fitness and nutrition app. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before you start a new exercise or eating plan.
